Brand Identity

Bluebirds in the Backyard During my internship at Bluebirds in the Backyard in Dordrecht, I was responsible for developing and shaping the company’s visual identity. I designed the logo and created a complete style guide to establish a consistent and recognizable look across different applications. I also translated the identity into various communication materials, including business cards and gift vouchers. These designs are still being used by Bluebirds in the Backyard today, which made this project a valuable experience in creating a visual identity that works both creatively and practically in a real-world setting.

“The goal was to create a recognizable and cohesive identity that captures the unique atmosphere of the café and vintage concept store. ”

Printed Businesscards & Giftvouchers

Physical printed matter was at the heart of this identity. I designed:

  • Arched Business Cards: Custom arch shape, unique and playfull.
  • Gift Vouchers: Folded gift voucher cards
  • Thank You Stationery: Arched cards that come with customers orders.

Warm Color System

The color palette draws from natural pigments: toasted almond silk, terracotta peach, deep mocha roast, and dusty lilac. These tones reinforce the feeling of an old family photo album or dried flower petals pressed inside a heavy vintage art book.
