The Challenge: Simplifying Safety Guidelines

Stammie is a safety mascot i created in commission for Arbo Begeleiding Stam, with a focus on making workplace safety more accessible and understandable. Helping workers understand workplace safety rules through simple and clear illustrations. The character is especially designed for people with low literacy, making important safety information more accessible without relying heavily on text. The goal of Stammie was to bridge the gap between compliance and engagement by creating an endearing, memorable safety mascot and translating critical workplace precautions into an interactive memory board game.

Instead of dry warnings, safety habits are learned through tactile gameplay, visual recognition, and friendly reinforcement.

“Making occupational health and safety memorable through playful character design and tactile game components.”

Mascot Development: Meet Stammie

I illustrated Stammie with warm, relatable facial features, aswell as a high-visibility orange safety vest, and an iconic yellow hardhat. His body language is welcoming rather than authoritarian — guiding workers through safe behaviors like a trusted, experienced colleague on the construction site.

Custom Ravensburger Memory Game Packaging

To bring the concept into reality, I designed custom gamecards for easy practice of the safety rules. The game consists of durable printed tiles featuring:

  • Hearing Protection: Stammie demonstrating the necessity of ear defenders in noisy machine zones.
  • Ergonomic Heavy Lifting: Visual guides contrasting safe bent-knee lifting posture versus risky back-bending poses.
  • Eye & Head Protection: Safety goggles and hardhat compliance icons paired with character reactions.
