About Me

Hi! My name is Anne van der Knaap. I am a 21-year-old Communication & Multimedia Design (CMD) Student in Breda.

I have always been interested in art and design. I like trying different ways of making things and working with different materials. I enjoy creating designs that tell a story, show an idea, or leave some room for people to make their own interpretation.

In 2021, I started studying at Grafisch Lyceum Rotterdam. There, I learned how to work with Adobe Creative Cloud and developed my skills in graphic design. I also got to explore more physical forms of art and design.

I have also worked on branding projects where I helped companies create their logo and visual style. I enjoy thinking about how a design can show what a company is about and make it easy for people to recognise.

After that, I started studying Communication & Multimedia Design in Breda in 2025. I worked on many different projects, from graphic design and branding to digital projects and concept development. I really enjoy being involved in the whole process, from coming up with an idea to creating the final design.

I still enjoy both digital and physical art. I like working with Adobe, but I also enjoy painting, sculpting, and trying out new materials. Outside of design, I get a lot of inspiration from music, film, makeup, and art.

I would describe myself as creative and curious. I like trying new things, learning from different projects, and turning ideas into something visual. Tell a story, communicate an idea, or leave room for interpretation.

“I like making things that feel a little strange, a little nostalgic, and impossible to ignore.”
— Anne vd Knaap
